GCC Trade Documents and Local Chemical Stock Guide

This page is a short reference for exporters, importers and procurement teams moving chemicals and commodities through the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Oman, Qatar, Kuwait and Bahrain. It covers two practical steps: finding material already held in the region, and preparing the documents that every shipment needs.

1. Check local stock before importing

Distributors in Dubai, Jebel Ali, Sharjah and Abu Dhabi often hold surplus solvents, acids, water-treatment chemicals, polymers and fuels. Buying locally can save weeks of lead time. Browse UAE local stock available to see current offers with grade, quantity and location.

2. Post what you need

If nothing matches, publish a free requirement with the specification, quantity, packing and delivery point. Suppliers review GCC buying requirements to find buyers for material they hold. Posts expire after seven days, so the list stays current.

3. Prepare the shipment documents

  • Commercial invoice – seller, buyer, description, HS code, quantity, value, currency and Incoterm. Use a free commercial invoice generator.
  • Packing list – packages, marks, net and gross weights and dimensions, matching the invoice. Try the packing list generator.
  • Bill of lading – send your forwarder a clean shipper's draft using a bill of lading draft template.
  • Certificate of analysis – batch test parameters and results for chemical cargo. Create one with the certificate of analysis (COA) generator.

All templates are listed on the free trade document generator page.

Before you ship

Confirm specifications, pricing basis and delivery terms with your counterparty, make sure weights and product names match across every document, and check requirements with your bank and the destination customs authority.
